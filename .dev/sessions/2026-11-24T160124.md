Administrators nominate and remove other administrators, manage global categories, and every operation the contract lists but the Controller does not carry out yet answers `501`.

- `apps/controller/src/administrators/`, `apps/controller/src/managementApi/`: once Usher has an administrator, any administrator nominates a person by subject, at most one pending nomination per nominee, and withdraws a pending one; until the first acceptance the first user acts exactly as before
- `apps/controller/src/administrators/`, `apps/controller/openapi.json`: `GET /admin/administrators` lists administrators and `DELETE /admin/administrators/{subject}` removes one, the caller included; removing the last is refused `409` `lastAdministrator`; a removal is its own row, revokes the removed administrator's pending nominations and is recorded as `admin.removal` at critical
- `apps/controller/src/eventLog/`, `apps/controller/src/managementApi/`: every change an administrator makes confirms their status in its own transaction under the administrators lock, held shared, and a change to who is an administrator holds it exclusively, so a change by a removed administrator never commits
- `apps/controller/src/migrations/`: migration 0015 re-keys administrators on the nomination accepted and adds `administrator_removals`; `firstAdministratorAccepted` leaves the contract
- `.dev/docs/deployed-stack-tests.md`: the assembled stack is tested end to end, nothing mocked, with a test administrator setting up and tearing down its fixtures
- `apps/controller/src/categories/`, `apps/controller/openapi.json`: `GET /admin/categories` lists every global category to administrators and counted owners, `POST /admin/categories` registers one, recorded as `category.creation` at warning, and `PATCH /admin/categories/{scopedName}` sets or clears its description, recorded as `category.descriptionChange` at info
- `apps/controller/src/migrations/`, `apps/controller/src/store/`, `apps/controller/src/grants/`: a category name keeps the letter case it is registered in and is held by one category in any letter case, by migration 0016's unique index; a concurrent registration of the same name in any case is answered `409` naming the category holding it, and updates and grants find a category in any letter case
- `modules/types/src/`: `isConcreteGlobalCategory` is exported, so the Controller judges a name by the payload contract's own rule
- `docs/deployment.md`: how an operator adds a global category, and that each catalogue's mapping names it in its registered spelling
