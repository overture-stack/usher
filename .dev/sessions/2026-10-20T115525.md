# 2026-10-20

## The onboarding page keeps its status sections

The developer's ruling: `docs/onboarding.md` stays as it is while nothing in it is out of date. Its Status box and "Where it stands" section were raised against the rule that documentation carries no progress status, and kept.

## Three roadmap items brought up to date with the Keycloak work

- Keycloak realm configuration: Overture configures Keycloak through Terraform's provider, from a module each deployment copies, rather than an operator under evaluation. Whether Usher's realm needs join that standard or ship as Usher's own module is open.
- The deployment runbook's break-glass bullet: Overture's deployment now documents who may run it and the events it relies on; a deployment of Usher elsewhere still has nothing of Usher's.
- Notification: whichever component sends it, it is not Keycloak, which holds no grants.

## The UAC flows mapped to epics and tickets

The iMS UAC user flows were mapped to the iMicroSeq project's epics and tickets, by build stage.

## The glossary defines policy

The glossary gains a Policy entry: the resources, their categories and the grants over them, as held in the policy store, kept apart from EGO's policies and from an organization's policy in the everyday sense.

## EGO's policy names read as an example

The admin model's migration steps now treat `STUDY-<id>` as an example: EGO's group and policy names are built from prefixes each deployment configures, and a study may have several.

## Roadmap: a reachability report

Future scope gains a reachability report: for one person and one resource, the records reached and the categories gating the rest, shown only to those who govern them, with the design in its atlas page.
