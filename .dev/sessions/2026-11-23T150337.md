The Bridge reads an Usher token's expiry on the Controller's clock, reports its own failures under their true cause, and renders `artifact.create` into a request's access; saved sets are designed to be reached as records are.

- `.dev/tech-debt.md`: the eight Controller test cases that are placeholders with no assertions are tracked, each by its source, until written
- `modules/bridge--express/src/core/`: an Usher token's life is read from the Controller's time at receipt, its `iat` plus one second plus the transit of the request that produced it, so clock skew can only shorten a token's life and the Bridge's wall clock decides no Usher token's expiry; a sign-in token's `exp` stays on the wall clock with its local tolerance
- `modules/bridge--express/src/core/`: the anonymous entry ends no later than the Controller's `exp` and is fetched again at 80% of its life, with the Bridge's clock ten minutes ahead or behind
- `modules/bridge--express/src/core/`, `modules/bridge--express/src/express/`: a throw while signing the channel assertion is reported as a defect on the exchange, the push and the poll alike; a throw from `core.resolve` is a failed resolve, a new answer stage covers throws while answering, and each request logs at most one `bridge.internalError`
- `scripts/`, `DEVELOPMENT.md`: `check:pack` checks the packed declarations, the packed JavaScript's imports, and that the tarballs installed together load through `require()` and `import()` at the root and at `./testing`; the install needs the npm registry for any dependency not already cached
- `.dev/design/`, `.dev/roadmap.md`: a saved set is reached as a record is, an owned set by its owners and those they share it with, a set with no owner through a base grant the instance sets; a set the reader cannot reach answers as a missing one, and saving one needs `artifact.create`; the share action, where sharing is recorded, the base grant's setting and default, and the administrator listing remain to design
- `modules/types/src/`, `modules/bridge--express/src/`: the request's access carries `artifactCreate` beside `results`, one enforcement result per registered key, rendered once per exchange by the same category machinery as record view; an adapter refuses a creation when any selected record falls outside it, and a catalogue open by configuration answers allow; the access value stays at version 1, so a consumer on this `usher-types` must not be mixed with the published `0.0.0-alpha.1` or `0.0.1-beta.1` Bridge
- `modules/bridge--express/src/core/`: `catalogue.renderingMismatch` and `catalogue.filterLimit` name the capability rendered, and the testing entry's access helpers take `{ artifactCreate, results }`, both required
