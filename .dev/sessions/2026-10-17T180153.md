# 2026-10-17

## Which clients may receive tokens for controlled data, recommended for the team

`delegated-access.md` carries two recommendations marked **Decide**, awaiting the developer's team. Each kind of client is decided for itself, and an undecided kind is refused in a deployment holding controlled data. A browser portal's backend holds the person's token there, under RFC 10017's backend-for-frontend pattern, since Arranger's components call the search server from the browser; this reaches Stage, the iMicroSeq portal and OHCRN's researcher interface, and the components' downloads.

## The MCP host's open questions have recommended answers

The MCP host's open questions were answered in the Arranger repository's plan and connection documents, settled where no team decision was needed and recommended where one is. The recommendations for the team are: which models may receive controlled data, retention of local copies, data crossing between connections, the browser-portal pattern, a gate on controlled data until those are settled, where the evaluation harness lives, the modules' licence, and the update channel's hosting and signing-key custody.
