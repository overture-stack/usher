Administrators revoke principals and sign out everyone, and the design documents catch up with the code.

- `apps/controller/src/revocations/`, `apps/controller/openapi.json`: an administrator revokes a principal with a recorded reason and lifts that revocation; a revoked administrator no longer counts as a live administrator but stays in the administrator listing, marked `revoked`, so another administrator can lift or remove them
- `apps/controller/src/revocations/`, `apps/controller/src/signInTokens/`: a sign-out of everyone, `POST /admin/sign-outs`, refuses every sign-in token issued before it, keeps every grant and ends no identity provider session; `keyCompromise` is not one of its reasons, since refusing earlier tokens does not answer a stolen signing key, and the planned revocation of a resource's users drops it as well
- `apps/controller/src/migrations/`, `modules/types/src/`, `modules/bridge--express/src/core/`: migration 0017 bounds a principal revocation's reasons, renames the global revocations table `everyone_sign_outs` and narrows its reasons, and renames the notice kind `global` to `signOutEveryone` on both sides of the wire
- `.dev/design/security-threat-model.md`: the sign-out of everyone's purpose and limits sit beside the principal revocation
- `.dev/design/`, `.dev/docs/`, `docs/`, `README.md`, `DEVELOPMENT.md`, `apps/controller/DEVELOPMENT.md`, `apps/controller/testing/README.md`: the documents agree with the code on who may be nominated an administrator, the custodian role, which Usher does not provide, the seeded roles and their permissions, what a suspended principal is served, the planned operations, which answer `501`, the Admin API being built, the append-only tables, the Controller's place in the repository and its tests, the token's members and lifetime, and the contract holding the Bridge's HTTP shapes
- `.dev/design/decisions.md`: the Bridge answering 503 past the grace period, and an Usher token's expiry read on each side's own clock, are marked superseded, each pointing to what replaced it
- `.dev/roadmap.md`: the adapter integration design item is removed, its contract, key distribution and payload versioning being built
- `docs/sops/`, `docs/deployment.md`, `apps/controller/openapi.json`, `.dev/design/glossary.md`: standard operating procedures (SOPs), one file per procedure, for setting up the first administrator, accepting a nomination, revoking and lifting a person, signing everyone out, and responding to a compromised identity provider signing key; deployment.md, the matching operations and the glossary link them, and "runbook" gives way to SOP throughout
- `docs/deployment.md`, `apps/controller/openapi.json`, `.dev/design/`, `modules/bridge--express/README.md`: behaviour the code had and no document gave, such as how long the identity provider's keys take to reach the Controller, a lowered sign-in token maximum applying to live sessions, the listing page sizes, the retention sweep's drain rate, the admission share's floor, and the Bridge's bounds and defaults, its response cap among them
