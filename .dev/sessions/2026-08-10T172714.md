## Ownership and stewardship model revision

Revised the ownership model: "owner" is no longer a separately assignable role. Ownership is now compound state (submitter + currently holds stewardship). Stewardship is granted at submission time and is transferable. Losing stewardship means the submitter reverts to submitter-only; they are no longer an "owner" in the governance sense. Consistent with OCAP requirements, since stewardship can be transferred to a First Nations community representative. Design documents updated accordingly.
