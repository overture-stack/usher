# 2026-09-13

One session running from 09-11 through 09-13. No file was opened on the first two days, so this
covers all three rather than splitting into three files with invented timestamps.

## The glossary now holds definitions, and nothing else

Writing rules that had accumulated inside definitions moved out to
`docs/atlas/roadmap/terminology-usage.md`. The defect they shared: a reader looking up a term met a
paragraph of guidance about how to write with it before reaching the sentence saying what it is. A
glossary answers what a word means; anything answering which word to write belongs to the agent-facing
atlas.

`glossary.md` gained a **Disambiguations** section, and the entries point into it rather than carrying
the collision themselves. The rule that decides placement: if the other meaning belongs to someone
else it goes below and is linked, and if it is our own distinction it stays in the entry. Six are
expanded there, including `relation`, which means a table in the relational model and an association
in entity-relationship modelling, and `subject`, which now has three meanings across OIDC, XACML and
CloudEvents.

## Vocabulary settled

- `resource key` split into **resource field** (the field) and **resource name** (its value), after it
  turned out to be doing both jobs plus colliding with cryptographic keys in three files.
- `data category` lost its prefix. Nothing else in the model carried one.
- `adopter` and `client app` became **application**, with **ushered application** where it matters that
  one has integrated Usher. `client` is reserved for a machine principal.
- `<noun> semantics` swept: 24 uses across 17 compounds, almost all meaning "the rule for X" or "how X
  behaves", with the compound hiding which.
- `user_group_members` became `group_users`. The old name put "member" on the belonging side, which is
  the collision the verb split exists to remove.
- `membership` survives for now. Three questions are open: whether it survives as a word, whether it
  flattens into a grant whose payload is a role, and whether `member` should exist at all given it
  confers nothing.

## The entity model

Eight entities, defined so that each stands without reaching for anything later in the list, and
ordered by what they build on rather than by when they came up. `glossary.md` carries the reading path
and the reason the list stops at seven: those describe **state**, while an invitation and an audit
entry record **process**. The test is whether removing it leaves the model able to say who may reach
what.

`permissions-model.md`'s entity schema is split along the same line.

Two definitions were wrong rather than merely loose. **Category** was "a named access dimension defined
at the platform level"; it is a collection of records defined by properties they carry, named per Usher
instance, with the properties settled per schema. **Capability** claimed a fixed vocabulary, which the
same session decided against.

## Decisions

- **A grant's expiry is resolved before the token is written**, and the token's `exp` is clamped to the
  earliest grant expiry it carries. Nothing watches the clock. This closed two `to-discuss.md` items
  that existed only because expiry had been routed through the revocation channel.
- **Embargo's recorded mechanism could not have worked.** It had a grant's expiry releasing data, and a
  grant ending removes access from its holder. Withdrawn in `permissions-model.md`, replaced by four
  requirements a working design must satisfy, post-MVP.
- **A custodian vacancy is a governance failure, not a system state.** A category exists before data
  carrying it can be submitted, so a live category with nobody governing it means a process was not
  followed. Three custodian questions are recorded as post-MVP alongside it.
- **Capabilities are defaults Usher ships and an instance may add to**, additively. Redefining a default
  is the unsafe move, because plugins check by name.
- **Choosing a resource's categories is part of creating it.** The chain of defaults is gone; a
  deployment may suggest and a person confirms. `open` is an ordinary choice rather than the absence of
  one.
- **Audit events follow CloudEvents**, with `source` carrying the emitting service. Event types are
  `[namespace.]entity.act`, and the vocabulary was renamed off the command voice. A capability and the
  event its exercise produces share an entity segment.

## RABAC alignment

`docs/atlas/roadmap/rabac-alignment.md` gained the capability model it was missing. Capabilities divide
by what they act on, along the control and data plane split already in `architecture.md`, with roles at
each plane and one token shape for both. The data-plane role is the missing first stage: the ceiling,
where the grant is the specific.

Also recorded there: attribute evaluation is split across two components, which is the one place Usher
departs from the source model structurally rather than by omitting a feature.

The plane names what a capability targets, not where it is enforced. Both are enforced in the control
plane, and the plugin is a control-plane component that runs inside an application, which is why
enforcing there does not put policy there.

## Two checks, and why they exist

`check-prose.sh` and `rank-references.py` are new.

The finding behind them: a convention an agent executes as a command holds, and the same agent's
knowledge of an equally clear rule does not. In one session, zero violations of the dash rule against
three of a defect class recorded that same morning, whose own entry specified the grep that would have
found it. The rules had become better at describing what to look for while nothing changed about when
anyone looked.

They are deliberately separate. The first fails on things wrong regardless of context; the second ranks
candidates a reader then judges. Merging them would poison the first, since a check emitting 150
candidates nobody reads is worse than no check.

Both found real defects in work from the same day: an event name violating a separator rule written
four hours earlier into the same file, and a pronoun whose nearer antecedent made the sentence
circular.

Two lessons that generalize went to `doc-review-patterns.md` rather than here, as classes M and N.
